# plan.md

1. **Option:** Curated vertical knowledge application & daily autonomous 6:00 PM coaching archive (**LacrosseTube**).
2. **User:** A dedicated lacrosse player, stick doctor, or coach who wants to master pocket stringing, Canadian box two-man mechanics, defensive slide packages, and wall-ball routines without wading through clickbait gear hauls or algorithmic vlog fluff.
3. **Build sentence:** When a player or coach searches `/`, clicks one of the 4 core skill pillars (or 10 drill sub-tags), or opens the Daily 6PM Top-10 Blog, the build displays human-vouched tutorials with 50% midpoint still frames (`hq2.jpg`) and printable PDF playbooks that open in a distraction-free modal and save to a local browser Locker (`localStorage`).
4. **Boundary:** Never display clickbait custom thumbnails (always use `hq2.jpg` 50% midpoint frames for videos), never require user accounts or cloud tracking, and never admit unverified links or non-lacrosse noise (such as "Buick LaCrosse" car manuals or Wisconsin municipal directories).
5. **Rejected idea:** An AI-generated synthetic lacrosse coaching chatbot with user login and social comment feeds—rejected because real field/box film, close-up sidewall knot demonstrations, and human-vouched PDF playbooks teach mechanics far better than unverified LLM text or noisy comment sections.
